Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Who we are

Apna Wallpaper is a free website for sharing phone wallpapers.

Information we collect

When a visitor uploads a wallpaper, we save the image, the title, the chosen category, and the name the visitor types (or "Anonymous" if empty). When a visitor sends a report, we save the wallpaper and the reason. We do not ask for passwords, phone numbers, or payment details.

How we use information

We use this information to show wallpapers on the site and to review reports.

Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on a visitor's previous visits to this site and other sites. Visitors can learn more and opt out at Google's ads settings page.

Remembered choices

The site saves the last category chosen on the upload page in the visitor's browser only.

Copyright and removal

If you own a wallpaper shown here and want it removed, contact us on the Contact page and we will remove it.

Changes

This policy may be updated, and the date at the top will change.
